How the blocker works

Small controls, right where you need them.

Redirect Blocker combines a list you control with a few built-in protections.

01

Add the sites you want to block

Enter one website host per line. The list starts empty and is yours to manage.

02

Cut down common interruptions

A short built-in ad-network list and popup suppression help reduce unwanted redirects and new tabs.

03

Pause whenever you need

Use the toolbar button to pause or resume blocks from your own website list.

Private by default

Your browsing is your business.

Your site list is stored in Chrome Sync. The extension doesn’t send URLs to an external server. Blocking coverage varies: it’s designed for your chosen hosts and a short list of common ad networks, so some redirects may still get through.
